# Contact & Support

We are committed to making Navigators the most reliable GNSS-denied tracking system in the world. Whether you've encountered a bug, have a feature request, or need help understanding your dashboard metrics, we're here to help.

## Technical Support
For issues relating to:
- The Android Application crashing or failing to launch.
- Sensor diagnostics showing inactive or failing hardware.
- Telemetry failing to sync to the Cloud Dashboard.

**Email:** support@navigators.com
*Please include your anonymous Device ID (found in the Settings menu) and a detailed description of what happened.*

## Feedback & Feature Requests
Did the AI speed model perform poorly in your specific vehicle? Do you want to see a new feature added to the Web Dashboard? Let us know!

**Email:** feedback@navigators.com

## Partnership Inquiries
Navigators can be integrated into fleet management systems, subterranean logistics operations, or integrated hardware stacks. For enterprise inquiries:

**Email:** enterprise@navigators.com
